How we used AI

Kokoro TTS (open-source, ran offline): https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro
  Turned our own written scripts into the 168 English voice lines (Mission Control, crew, cutscene narration).
Free Text to Speech Online: https://www.text-to-speech.online/en/
  Turned our own written lines into extra spoken audio.
Claude Code (Anthropic): https://claude.com/claude-code
  AI coding assistant for the TypeScript code, automated tests, build/publishing setup and documents. The team decided what to build and tested every mission.
